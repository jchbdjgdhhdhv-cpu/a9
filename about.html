About Hemwork

Small alterations, big difference.

Hemwork is an independent fashion website about hems, hemlines and length.

We think length is the most overlooked part of fit. A good hem makes inexpensive clothes look made for you; a poor one spoils the best. Our guides explain the choices in plain language, and our tools help you see and measure them.

We do not sell clothes, run a tailoring service or take payment for mentions. Every guide is checked by a second editor and dated.

— measured twice, written once
